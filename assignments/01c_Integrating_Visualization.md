# Assignment 1c: Integrating Visualization into Your Career Strategy

**Course:** MTEC 4502 – Career and Portfolio Seminar  
**Instructor:** Dr. David B. Smith  
**Due:** Week 4

## Objective

Translate your written Strategic Framework (Part 1b) into visual and comparative evidence about your career direction. This week emphasizes analysis: understanding which job sites are useful for your specific career identity, identifying your current position, and visualizing the relationship between your strengths and the skills the market requires. Process-planning visuals such as Gantt charts and timeline roadmaps are optional extensions, not required deliverables for this stage.

## Assignment Steps

### Step 1: Research Visualization Platforms
1. **Explore Platforms:** Research at least 3 visualization tools (e.g., MindMeister, Lucidchart, Miro, Trello, Coggle, Draw.io, Canva).
2. **Deliverable (150–200 words):** Write a brief platform comparison evaluating ease of use, design flexibility, collaboration features, and accessibility (free vs. paid tiers). State which platform you chose and why.

### Step 2: Complete the Required Analysis Visuals

Create both of the following required visuals:

* **Skills-Gap Radar / Chart:** Compare your current proficiency against target requirements drawn from your job-market research. Label the dimensions and explain the scale.
* **SWOT Matrix:** Evaluate the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats affecting your current career direction.

You may also create a Career Pathway Diagram, concept map, or another visual form. A Gantt chart or timeline roadmap is optional because this week focuses on analysis rather than process scheduling.

### Step 3: Produce Initial Visual Rough Draft
Create an initial visual draft incorporating:
* The evidence from your job-site and advertisement research
* Major skill gaps identified in Part 1b
* Your SWOT analysis
* Your Skills-Gap Radar / Chart
* Key tools, resources, and opportunities relevant to your career identity
