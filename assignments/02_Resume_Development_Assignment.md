# Assignment 2: Résumé Development & Analysis

**Course:** MTEC 4502 – Career and Portfolio Seminar  
**Instructor:** Dr. David B. Smith  
**Due:** Week 6 (Draft) / Week 7 (Final Revision)  

---

## Objective

Gather, evaluate, and structure your professional background into a polished 1-page resume tailored to your target industry sector. This assignment is not a standalone document: it should build directly on the strategic plan and visualization work from the earlier part of the course. Continue to refine your radar chart, competency map, and professional narrative so that the resume reflects highly specific capabilities, not just a general interest area.

Use this project to translate your earlier thinking into a more actionable professional identity. In other words, the resume should reflect the competencies that matter in your target field, the evidence you already have, and the skills you still need to develop.

---

## Assignment Steps

### 1. Material Gathering & Summary Statement
* Compile education, relevant work experience, internships, technical projects, certifications, and skills.
* Draft a 2–3 sentence **Professional Summary** drawn from your Assignment 1 career strategy.
* Revisit your radar chart or strategic visualization and identify the core competencies that are most relevant to your target field.

### 2. Resume Comparative Analysis
* Research 2 real-world resumes from professionals in your field (or public resume databases).
* Identify 1 effective ("good") resume and 1 ineffective ("bad") resume.
* Write a **1–2 paragraph analysis** explaining why each resume succeeds or fails in formatting, readability, conciseness, and clarity.

### 3. Refine Your Career Competency Map
* Build on the visualization work from the earlier assignments (1c–1e) by making your chart more specific and actionable.
* For each major competency or capability, define the **observable skill or capability** that would be assessed.
* Create a simple rubric or Likert-style scale that asks: **What exactly would someone need to be able to do to score a given level on this competency?**
* Connect these competency statements to your current strengths, gaps, and next steps in education or project development.
* This should help turn your radar chart from a descriptive image into a practical tool for continued growth.

### 4. Draft Your 1-Page Resume
* Build a clean 1-page draft adhering to single-page precision guidelines.
* Ensure clear sections: Header/Contact, Summary, Technical Skills, Projects, Experience, Education.
* Write your resume language so it reflects the specific capabilities and professional signals represented in your competency map.

### 5. Action Plan for Continued Growth
* Identify 2–3 priority competencies that are still underdeveloped.
* Name the evidence or experience that would help you improve them.
* Explain how this connects to your target job, industry expectations, and next phase of learning.
