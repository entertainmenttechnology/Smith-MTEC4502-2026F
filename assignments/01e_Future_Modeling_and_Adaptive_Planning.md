## Overview

This assignment continues from **Assignment 1d (AI Collaboration and Career Futures)** and revisits the visual map you first built in **Assignment 1c (Integrating Visualization)**.

In this version of the assignment, you will move beyond a single visual map and construct a more specific, assessable, and recursive model of your career direction. Instead of only showing general strengths and interests, you will develop a **role-level competency radar chart** and then break selected axes into more specific sub-radars. This lets you move from broad strategic thinking to a measurable system for skill development, action planning, and future adaptation.

This is a more advanced form of visualization and planning. It is designed to help you answer a crucial question: not just what you want to do, but what specific capabilities you need to build, assess, and refine in order to move toward that future.

## Assignment Components

### Part 1: Future of Work Stress Test Synthesis (500–750 Words)
Write a synthesis answering:
* How is your target role projected to evolve, hybridize, or fragment over the next 5–10 years?
* Which current skills may become obsolete or automated? Which emerging competencies will become essential?
* Which parts of your current strategy are vulnerable, and which are most robust?
* Cite at least **one Core reading** and **two independent Zotero sources**.

### Part 2: Role-Level Radar Chart
Create a radar chart for your target role, discipline, or job family. This chart should include **5–8 major competencies** that are essential to that direction.

Examples of axes might include:
* Creative Problem Solving
* Technical Execution
* Research and Evidence Use
* Collaboration and Teamwork
* Communication
* Production Workflow
* Adaptability and Iteration
* Portfolio/Professional Evidence

Your chart should not simply list broad categories. Each axis must be defined in specific, observable terms.

### Part 3: Recursive Sub-Radar Analysis
Select **one or two** of the most important axes from your role-level chart and break each into a second, more specific radar chart.

This second chart should show the internal components of that larger competency.

Examples:
- **Main role:** Game Designer
  - **Major axis:** Technical Design
  - **Sub-axes:** Systems Thinking, Prototyping, Playtesting, Balance, Collaboration with Engineers
- **Main role:** Technical Artist
  - **Major axis:** 3D Production
  - **Sub-axes:** Modeling, Retopology, UV Mapping, Texturing, Asset Optimization, Pipeline Workflow
- **Main role:** Media Producer
  - **Major axis:** Workflow and Coordination
  - **Sub-axes:** Scheduling, Asset Management, Communication, Budgeting, Quality Control

This recursive breakdown is the key part of the assignment. It shows that a broad competency is actually a system of more precise skills that can be measured, improved, and evaluated.

### Part 4: Competency Rubric and Assessment Framework
For each major axis and each recursive sub-axis, create a simple rubric that defines what performance looks like across a scale.

Use a scale such as:
* 1 = Not yet demonstrated
* 2 = Emerging
* 3 = Basic competence
* 4 = Strong and consistent
* 5 = Advanced / can teach or lead

For each competency, describe:
* the competency being assessed,
* what a student would need to do to achieve each level,
* the evidence that would support the rating,
* and what action would move the student to the next level.

This section should answer the question: **What exactly would someone need to be able to do to score a given level on this competency?**

### Part 5: Adaptive Planning and Growth Action Plan
Based on your chart and rubric, identify:
* your strongest competencies,
* your most vulnerable competencies,
* the competencies that matter most for your next step,
* and the actions you will take to improve them.

Your final explanation should describe how your strategy changed in response to future-of-work analysis and how the radar chart can guide your continued education, skill-building, and professional development.

## Assessment Criteria

Your work will be evaluated on the clarity and usefulness of your radar chart, not just its visual design. Strong submissions will:
* identify the right competencies for a specific target role,
* define those competencies in observable, measurable language,
* create a meaningful recursive breakdown,
* generate a credible rubric that connects to evidence,
* and turn the chart into an action plan for future learning and professional growth.

This assignment aims to help you build a map that is not only descriptive, but operational: a tool for evidence-based career development.
