# Assignment 1f: AI Collaboration Analysis & Critical Reflection

**Course:** MTEC 4502 – Career and Portfolio Seminar  
**Instructor:** Dr. David B. Smith  
**Due:** Week 6  

---

## Objective

Evaluate how Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools were integrated as collaborative partners across the research, ideation, drafting, and visualization stages of Assignment 1 as a whole (1a–1e). This is a cumulative reflection, distinct from the specific AI exchange documented in Assignment 1d. The goal is to critically assess AI-generated output, identify limitations or hallucinations, and document your methodology while maintaining full authorial integrity.

---

## Assignment Components

### 1. AI Interaction & Process Log
Document all AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Grammarly, Midjourney) used during Assignment 1. For each tool/session, record:
* **Tool & Version** (e.g., ChatGPT 4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet)
* **Purpose & Stage** (e.g., brainstorming future roles, drafting resume summary, formatting markdown)
* **Prompts Used:** Exact text of key prompts.
* **Outputs & Editing:** How much of the output was kept, discarded, or rewritten.

### 2. Critical Evaluation & Reflection (1–2 Pages / 400–600 Words)
Write a critical reflection addressing:
* **Benefits:** How did AI support ideation, surface blind spots, or streamline workflow?
* **Limitations & Hallucinations:** Where did AI provide inaccurate, overly generic, or misleading information? How did you verify facts?
* **Authorial Control & Integrity:** How did you ensure your personal voice and original judgment remained dominant?

### 3. Ethical Consideration
State your adherence to CUNY's Academic Integrity policy regarding AI use, confirming proper attribution of non-original text or media. *(Note: If you chose NOT to use AI for Assignment 1, state your reasoning clearly here. No grade penalty applies).*

---

## Deliverables

1. **AI Process & Prompt Log** (or completed course AI log form).
2. **Critical Reflection Essay (400–600 words).**
